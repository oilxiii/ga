Beta10 English + Bugfix build beta10-en2

Fixed all eight confirmed findings from the review:
1. Cancelled or reopened Dash previews restore Primary Action state. An unpaid
   Dash cannot end an activation and strand a unit on an old Timeline slot.
2. When human Char confirms Dash with Char Kick, the kick resolves before enemy
   movement Responses such as Iron Grip. Costs and pickups commit first. If the
   Enforcer survives, Iron Grip can still resolve. Garrison kicks use the same order.
3. Disarm suppresses Hero Gundam's Vulcan bonus Critical dice. The dice overlay
   does not wait for a suppressed bonus roll. AI damage estimates match.
4. Disarmed AoE attacks retain normal bonuses, including Breast Fire's adjacent
   Damage +2. Printed Critical effects remain disabled; rolled Critical results
   and independent Critical-counting bonuses remain intact.
5. Declaring Tail Blade against a Garrison enables Annihilate, just as a Unit attack
   does. Annihilate still needs Energy and a legal Rex Claws follow-up target.
6. Drive Them Back's follow-up Damage 1 only affects a target still on the board.
   A target defeated by Collision returns to Reserve at full HP as normal.
7. A Unit finishing Push or Pull picks up Energy/Upgrades on the final hex, before
   movement Responses. Passing through items, zero movement and Garrisons do not
   collect items.
8. Epic Shot's Range 3 allied Strength bonus now requires Line of Sight in both
   combat calculation and AI evaluation.
